About

One builder, one connected system

Nexoria is the brand behind everything I build — a solo-developer ecosystem of websites, tools, and software that all share one identity instead of living as disconnected side projects.

Nexorealm.org exists to make that ecosystem legible: what's live, what each project actually does, and where to find it — all pulled from the source automatically instead of copy-pasted and left to rot.

New projects get added to the orbit as they ship. This hub grows with them.
